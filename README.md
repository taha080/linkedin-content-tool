# Founder's LinkedIn Week

Plan a week of founder LinkedIn posts in minutes. Hook first, story second,
lesson last — no pitch, no spam.

**Live:** https://taha080.github.io/linkedin-content-tool/

## What it does

- Enter your niche/topic, audience and tone, pick your posting days
- Generates a full week's worth of ready-to-post LinkedIn drafts
- 5 rotating post types: personal story & lesson, contrarian take, how-to list,
  mistake & fix, behind the scenes
- Week calendar (Mon–Sun), copy per post, copy all, download as `.txt`
- Regenerate any single post for a fresh angle
- Optional: paste your own free Gemini API key for AI drafts (stored only in
  your browser — never sent anywhere except Google's Gemini API). No key
  needed: the built-in templates work on their own.

## Built on real ghostwriting practice

The templates follow the style of a working LinkedIn ghostwriter for
founders: hook in the first line, short readable lines, story + lesson, a
question at the end, 2–3 relevant hashtags. No pitch, no spam.

## Free version

This is the free version: 3 or 5 posts per week from built-in templates.
A paid version ($5/month) with unlimited posts, saved weeks and AI drafts
is planned.

## Run it yourself

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host.
No build step, no backend, no keys required.
